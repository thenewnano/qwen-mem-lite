## Approach

A single merge of `upstream/main` into `main` (`PORTING.md`), same as the three
earlier passes. 63 files conflicted. The dominant collision was the fork's own
rebrand against the upstream rename of the CLI-invocation surface to the path
based `CLI_INVOKE` / `shellWord` helpers (D#462): every `qwen-mem-lite <cmd>`
hint upstream had rewritten became a conflict. Where the file's only fork
change was the rebrand the resolution took upstream's logic and re-applied the
fork identity; where the fork has real logic (the steering layouts, the OpenAI
compatible LLM leg, install/update identity) the merge kept the fork and folded
upstream's new behaviour in by hand. Upstream's whole new files were adopted
with the rebrand applied. Dependency pins were upgraded during the merge (take
upstream's newest pins, `npm install` to regenerate `package-lock.json`), per
the SKILL.md rule.
