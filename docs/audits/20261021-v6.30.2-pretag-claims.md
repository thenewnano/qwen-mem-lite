## FALSE and MISLEADING, ranked

**P2** (a user or maintainer would act wrongly on it)
1. #1: README (both languages) and the CHANGELOG title say "never removed while another session holds the store". Under root (containers), CAP_DAC_OVERRIDE or a filesystem that ignores modes, the WAL is renamed under a live session, and the "Memory off instead of a delete" bullet does not apply there. Such a user would believe a concurrent session is protected.
2. #6: README, zh and doctor say the copies "hold the commits after the damaged frame". They also hold every un-checkpointed commit before it, which the store no longer has, and they cannot be opened as they are. A user told to "delete them once you no longer need them" underestimates what they are deleting.

**P3**
3. #3: the causal story "retried on a database file that was damaged too" is wrong on the fixture cited. The main file was intact when the retry failed (SHORT_READ, 20/20) and was damaged at exit. This sits in the CHANGELOG, bcbe1f51 and the schema.mjs docblock, so it points maintainers at the wrong mechanism.
4. #2: "the message says to close the other sessions" appears only in the MCP server's stderr and in the CLI. The hooks and SessionStart say nothing.
5. #4: "damaged … 20 of 20" for 6.30.1 is not stable (my two runs: 11/20 and 20/20). It is stated without the caveat d506 recorded.
6. #16: the update prune did not delete the example hook. It needs the launcher shape.
7. #5: "lost all of them" and "lost what the other process had committed" overstate one direction of a loss that depends on exit order.
8. #11: cf0c1c4c says each new test was red on the old code. "No WAL on disk" is green on both older trees.
9. #25: "6.8 (6.0 before, about 8% slower)" puts an absolute-time ratio next to a multiple that rose 13%.
10. #18: the setup.sh warning lists events, not "exactly the hooks".
11. #13: server.mjs:258 says no -wal is left, but a 0-byte one is.
