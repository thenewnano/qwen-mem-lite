## FALSE

### F1. "4.5 多出来的 kept lesson 大多是 GENERIC 或 WRONG"

- **出处**：`docs/measurement/rulers.md:70`："4.5's extra kept lessons are mostly quote-then-platitude (GENERIC) or quote-then-reversed-claim (WRONG) — grounding passes on the quote, not on the claim built on it"。提交 `b98eb3c1`："4.5's extra lessons quote a DIAGNOSIS line, which passes grounding, then add a platitude or reverse the claim."
- **证据**：用 key + verdicts 统计 4.5 臂 40 条 kept 里不是"两判 ACCURATE"的 24 条，其标签对如下：

| 标签对 | 条数 |
|---|---|
| PARTLY+PARTLY | 12 |
| ACCURATE+PARTLY | 4 |
| WRONG+WRONG | 4 |
| GENERIC+GENERIC | 3 |
| GENERIC+WRONG | 1 |

  - 以 PARTLY 为最差标签的有 16/24，GENERIC 或 WRONG 只有 8/24。
  - 与 5.5 臂比（9 条非 ACCURATE：PARTLY 6、WRONG 2、GENERIC 1），4.5 多出的部分是 PARTLY +10、GENERIC/WRONG +5。
  - 抽样 8 条 4.5 PARTLY+PARTLY 的判词，都是同一个形状："quote supported; … mechanism/remedy … not shown / invented"（例如 L014、L036、L062、L070）。也就是引用成立，然后加了证据里没有的原因或修法，**不是**套话，也**不是**结论反转。
- **改为**：
  > 4.5's non-accurate kept lessons (24 of 40) are mostly PARTLY (16): the quoted DIAGNOSIS line holds, then the lesson adds a cause or remedy the evidence does not show. 8 are WRONG or GENERIC, against 3 of 5.5's 9 — grounding passes on the quote, not on what is built on it.
- 提交 `b98eb3c1` 已在本地，只能在 rulers.md 里改正。CHANGELOG 没有复述这句。
